Welcome — why small apps instead of one big one

July 26, 2026

MirodaLab is dedicated to building small useful apps - but why concentrate on small apps?

Big software companies produce enormous software suites with an extensive range of possibilities. This is fine if you use these apps every day, and have a good oversight of all the functions that are available. However most of us don't use these software suites to their full potential, because we simply have no need of all the functions that they afford us. Not only that, but these suites are so large that it is almost impossible to have a complete grasp of how to do something that you haven't done before - how can you find the one command you need from the hundreds of collapsed menus that they offer? If you're like me, you spend twenty minutes googling to try and figure it out, only to find that it doesn't do exactly the one thing that you want, unless you learn about another twenty functions that are required first.

So, small apps! Easy to understand. Do what it says on the tin, and nothing else.
