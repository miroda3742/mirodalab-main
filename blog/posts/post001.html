We're underway! In the Margins is available on Google Play

August 26, 2026

Moving these apps from the testing arena to publication is a long process, but today I am happy to say that the first app, In the Margins, is available on Google Play.

If you read, then this app is the perfect companion. It allows you to track your reading and build your library. That's it. No social media site, so no trying to keep up with the Joneses - just read it and record it. There are no adverts, there's no subscription. We hope you enjoy it 😊!
