Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Overview

This policy covers the MirodaLab website and blog at this domain. Each app — Margins, CleanSlate, Freeflow, and Plotbench — has its own Privacy Policy on its own subdomain, since each app handles data differently.

Information We Collect

This site does not run analytics or advertising trackers by default. If you leave a comment on a blog post, that comment is handled by Giscus and stored as a GitHub Discussion in this project's repository, subject to GitHub's own privacy policy. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How We Use Information

Any information you provide — through a comment or an email — is used only to respond to you, moderate discussion, or maintain the site. We do not sell or share it with third parties for advertising.

Data Storage & Security

This site is static and hosted through a standard web host (e.g. Cloudflare Pages via GitHub). Comments live in CloudFlare and GitHub's infrastructure, not ours. We don't operate our own database of visitor data.

Third-Party Services

Hosting provider and Giscus/GitHub Discussions for comments. Update this section if you add analytics, a mailing list, or other third-party embeds.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
